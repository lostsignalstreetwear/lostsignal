CUSTOMER CARE

RETURN
POLICY.

Every piece is hand printed to order, so returns are handled with care.

RETURN WINDOW
Return requests must be submitted within 15 days from the date the order is delivered. Eligible items must be unworn, unwashed, unaltered, free from stains, odors, makeup, pet hair, or other signs of use, in original condition, and with original tags attached when applicable. Returns submitted after the 15-day period may be refused, except where required by applicable law.

REFUNDS
Approved refunds are based on the actual amount paid by the customer. Returned merchandise must be received and inspected before a refund is approved. Lost Signal does not automatically approve every return. Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to an error by Lost Signal or a defective or incorrect item. Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

DISCOUNTED ORDERS
Items purchased using a discount code or coupon are still subject to the 15-day return policy unless the specific promotion states otherwise. Refunds for discounted items are based on the actual amount paid after the discount was applied. The coupon or discount value is not refundable as cash. If returning an item causes the remaining items in the order to no longer qualify for the original discount, promotion, or minimum purchase requirement, Lost Signal may recalculate the discount when determining the refund amount. Customers will not receive a refund based on the original full retail price when the item was purchased at a discounted price.

EXCHANGES
Eligible items may be exchanged within the 15-day return period, subject to availability. Customers are responsible for applicable return or exchange shipping costs when the exchange is due to incorrect size selection, change of mind, or ordering the wrong item. Lost Signal will cover appropriate shipping costs when the exchange is required because Lost Signal sent the wrong or defective item.

DAMAGED / DEFECTIVE / INCORRECT
Contact Lost Signal within 15 days of delivery if you receive a damaged item, defective item, wrong item, wrong color, or a significant printing defect. Customers may be required to provide photographs of the issue. Lost Signal will review the issue and determine whether a replacement, exchange, or refund is appropriate.

SCREEN PRINTING
Minor variations in ink texture, print placement, and print appearance may naturally occur with screen printing. Minor variations do not automatically constitute a defect. Significant printing defects will be reviewed by Lost Signal.

NON-RETURNABLE ITEMS
Worn clothing, washed clothing, altered clothing, customer-damaged clothing, clothing with stains, odors, makeup, pet hair, or other signs of use, items returned after the 15-day return period, clearly identified final-sale items, and custom or personalized items are not eligible for return except when defective or incorrectly produced.

SHIPPING
Lost Signal ships exclusively via UPS. Available shipping services and costs may vary depending on customer location, package weight, package dimensions, shipping service selected, carrier availability, and other applicable shipping factors. Shipping costs are generally the responsibility of the customer unless Lost Signal specifically offers free shipping or a shipping promotion. The actual shipping cost will be displayed to the customer during checkout before the order is completed.

RETURNS / EXCHANGES SHIPPING
For returns or exchanges caused by customer preference, incorrect size selection, change of mind, or ordering the wrong item, the customer is responsible for applicable return shipping costs. For defective, damaged, or incorrectly fulfilled orders caused by Lost Signal, Lost Signal will cover appropriate return or replacement shipping costs.

SHIPPING TIMES
Processing time is the time needed to prepare and package an order before shipment. Shipping or transit time is the time the package is in transit after it has been handed to the carrier. USPS and UPS delivery times can vary because of weather, holidays, carrier delays, incorrect addresses, and other circumstances outside Lost Signal's control. Lost Signal does not guarantee delivery dates unless the selected shipping service provides a guaranteed delivery date.

EXCEPTIONS
We offer a full refund or replacement for damaged, incorrect, or defective orders. Contact us within 15 days of delivery with photos and a description of the issue. Each claim is reviewed individually to determine the appropriate resolution.
